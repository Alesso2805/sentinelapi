# SentinelAPI — backlog and sprint plan

Oct 22, 2026 · @Alessandro David

42 stories in 6 epics (E0 to E5). E0 records what was built on August 31, 2026, retroactively, so that the audit can check each criterion against a test; SEN-09 is the documentation of October 22. E1 to E5 come from the audit of the same day (`docs/AUDIT.md`, kept locally since SEN-38; the README's Known limitations sums it up): every blocking or important finding is a story. The rest come from the README's roadmap (migrations, a dashboard, notifications on CRITICAL events) and from what the portfolio needs to link the project: a public repository and a live demo. MoSCoW priority (M = Must, S = Should, C = Could) and estimate in points (1, 2, 3, 5, 8).

**Correctness before exposure** (proposal, 2026-10-22). SentinelAPI is a security library: a public demo with a known bypass would be worse than no demo. So the detectors are fixed (Sprint 2) and the audit trail made private (Sprint 3) before anything is deployed (Sprint 5).

**The repository is public now** (product owner, 2026-10-22). SEN-10 and SEN-38 move to Sprint 0 instead of waiting for 1.0.0. So that nothing the repository claims is untrue in the meantime, its README states the project's status and lists the audit's findings as known limitations, each with its story. The demo still waits for E2.

## Sprint plan

**No calendar** (product owner, 2026-10-22): the sprints run back to back, each closing as soon as its stories are merged, as fast as they can go. The dates below were the original two-week plan and are kept only for reference. On the same day the product owner accepted ADR-0004 to ADR-0010 and the proposed rules (AR-16 to AR-18), and asked for each story's PR to be merged as soon as its CI is green. Each sprint closes with a demonstrable goal and its notes in `docs/sprints/sprint-N.md`.

| Sprint | Dates | Goal | Stories | Pts |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 0 | Aug 31 – Oct 25, 2026 | Baseline as built, documented, audited, and public under version control | SEN-01 to 10, 38 | 25 |
| 1 | Oct 26 – Nov 8 | Green from a clean clone, CI that blocks regressions | SEN-11 to 16 | 12 |
| 2 | Nov 9 – 22 | Detectors that hold: the same windows everywhere, limits that mean what they say, no cheap evasion | SEN-17 to 21 | 19 |
| 3 | Nov 23 – Dec 6 | A three-line integration and an audit trail that is migrated, private and flood-proof | SEN-22 to 26 | 18 |
| 4 | Dec 7 – 20 | A production-ready stack and the attack console | SEN-27 to 32 | 18 |
| 5 | Dec 21, 2026 – Jan 3, 2027 | A deployed demo and release 1.0.0 | SEN-33 to 37 | 14 |

**Order inside Sprint 1.** SEN-11 first (nothing installs reproducibly without it; its first step is #1), then SEN-12, SEN-13, SEN-14, SEN-15, and SEN-16 last, so the cleanup runs against green checks.

**Order inside Sprint 2.** SEN-17 first: its contract suite and injectable clock are what the other stories' tests stand on. Then SEN-19 before SEN-18, because SEN-19 changes the key that SEN-18's limits count on. Then SEN-20 and SEN-21. ADR-0004 and ADR-0005 must be accepted before SEN-19 and SEN-20 start.

**Order inside Sprint 3.** SEN-22 first (the installer is where the audit sink plugs in), then SEN-23, SEN-24, SEN-25, and SEN-26.

**Order inside Sprint 4.** SEN-27, SEN-28, SEN-29, SEN-30, then SEN-32 (it needs SEN-26's events API), and SEN-31 last.

**Order inside Sprint 5.** SEN-33 first (it needs a server), then SEN-34, SEN-35, SEN-36, and SEN-37 last, whose README for visitors shows the deployed demo. SEN-38 was done in Sprint 0.

**Buffer.** If a sprint does not close, move out the "Should" stories (SEN-16, SEN-30, SEN-31, SEN-34, SEN-35, SEN-36) before cutting quality on a "Must". Never deploy (SEN-33) with an E2 story open: a public security demo with a known bypass is worse than no demo.

**Minimum ceremonies.** 30-minute planning at the start of each sprint, a 5-minute demo at the close and a 3-line retrospective in `docs/sprints/sprint-N.md`.
