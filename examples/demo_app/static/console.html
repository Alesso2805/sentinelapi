Attacks

  • Burst

    Seventy requests in a moment from one address, against a limit of 60 per minute.

  • Credential stuffing

    One account, one wrong password from each of 20 addresses, so no address fails twice. Then its owner signs in with the right password.

  • Token replay

    The owner redeems a single-use refresh token; then a stolen copy is replayed from another address.
